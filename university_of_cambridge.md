## UK {#uk}

### University of Cambridge {#university-of-cambridge}

**Website:** [University of Cambridge](https://www.undergraduate.study.cam.ac.uk/applying)

#### Admissions Requirements

- Academic Requirements:
-   • A strong academic record, typically with A*AA or AAA at A-level, or equivalent international qualifications.
-   • Meeting the specific subject requirements for your chosen course.
-   • Typically, a GPA of 3.7 or higher (on a 4.0 scale) or equivalent.
-   • Meeting the English language proficiency, typically IELTS 7.5 or TOEFL 110.
- Standardized Tests:
-   • Sixth Term Exam Papers (STEP) for Mathematics and Computer Science courses.
-   • University Clinical Aptitude Test (UCAT) for Medicine and Veterinary Medicine courses.
-   • Law National Aptitude Test (LNAT) for Law courses.
-   • Engineering and Science Admission Test (ESAT) for Engineering and Natural Sciences courses.
-   • Mathematics Admission Test (TMUA) for Mathematics courses.
- Application Materials:
-   • UCAS application, including a personal statement and academic reference.
-   • My Cambridge Application, including a transcript and any required written work or portfolios.
-   • College admission assessments, if required.
- International Requirements:
-   • International financial support, including tuition fees and living costs.
-   • Visas and immigration requirements.
-   • Language proficiency tests, such as IELTS or TOEFL.
- Additional Requirements:
-   • Contextual data, including information about your school and socio-economic background.
-   • Disclosure of unspent criminal convictions.
-   • Providing a transcript and disruption to your studies, if applicable.
-   • Mature student applications, if applicable.
-   • Second undergraduate degree applications, if applicable.
- Post-Application Requirements:
-   • Written work and portfolios, if required.
-   • Cambridge interviews, if invited.
-   • College admission assessments, if required.
- After You Apply:
-   • Application decisions and outcomes.
-   • Terms of Admission, including any conditions or requirements.
-   • Reconsideration eligibility criteria, if applicable.

#### Application Deadlines

**Ucas Deadline:** October 15, 2025 for Oxford, Cambridge, medicine, veterinary science, and dentistry. January 15, 2026 for most other courses.

**International Deadline:** Same as UCAS deadlines, though some universities have different deadlines for international students.
