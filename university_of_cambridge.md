## UK {#uk}

### University of Cambridge {#university-of-cambridge}

**Website:** [University of Cambridge](https://www.undergraduate.study.cam.ac.uk/applying)

#### Admissions Requirements

- Academic requirements:
-   • A strong academic record, with high grades in relevant subjects
-   • Typically, A*AA or AAA at A-level, or equivalent qualifications
-   • Minimum GPA of 3.7 or higher (for international students)
-   • Some courses may require specific subjects or grades
- Standardized test scores:
-   • No general entrance exams required, but some courses may require specific tests (e.g., STEP, LNAT, ESAT, TMUA)
- English language proficiency:
-   • IELTS: minimum overall score of 7.5, with at least 7.0 in each component
-   • TOEFL: minimum overall score of 110, with at least 25 in each component
-   • Cambridge English: Proficiency or Advanced, with a minimum overall score of 191
- Application materials:
-   • UCAS application, including personal statement and academic reference
-   • My Cambridge Application, including additional information and written work (if required)
-   • Written work or portfolio (if required for specific courses)
-   • Transcripts and certificates from previous education
- Interviews and assessments:
-   • Some courses may require interviews or assessments as part of the application process
-   • These may be held in person or online, depending on the course and circumstances
- International students:
-   • International financial support may be available
-   • Visas and immigration requirements must be met
-   • Year abroad opportunities may be available for some courses
- Additional requirements:
-   • Contextual data may be considered in the application process
-   • Unspent criminal convictions must be disclosed
-   • Admissions complaints procedure is available if needed

#### Application Deadlines

**Ucas Deadline:** October 15, 2025 for Oxford, Cambridge, medicine, veterinary science, and dentistry. January 15, 2026 for most other courses.

**International Deadline:** Same as UCAS deadlines, though some universities have different deadlines for international students.
