## UK {#uk}

### University of Cambridge {#university-of-cambridge}

**Website:** [University of Cambridge](https://www.undergraduate.study.cam.ac.uk/applying)

#### Admissions Requirements

- Academic Requirements:
-   • A strong academic record, typically with A*AA or AAA at A-level, or equivalent qualifications.
-   • Meeting the specific subject requirements for your chosen course.
-   • GPA: Typically 3.7 or higher (on a 4.0 scale) for international students.
- Standardized Tests:
-   • Sixth Term Exam (STEP) for Mathematics and Computer Science courses.
-   • Clinical aptitude test (UCAT) for Medicine courses.
-   • Law test (LNAT) for Law courses.
-   • Engineering and Science test (ESAT) for Engineering and Natural Sciences courses.
-   • Mathematics test (TMUA) for Mathematics and Computer Science courses.
- Application Materials:
-   • UCAS application, including a personal statement.
-   • My Cambridge Application, including additional information and academic achievements.
-   • Written work or portfolio (if required for your course).
-   • Transcripts and certificates from previous academic institutions.
- Language Proficiency:
-   • English language proficiency, typically IELTS 7.5 or TOEFL 110.
- Financial Information:
-   • Tuition fees and living costs information.
-   • Financial support and scholarship applications.
- International Student Requirements:
-   • International entry requirements, including qualifications and language proficiency.
-   • Visa and immigration information.
-   • International financial support and scholarship applications.
- Additional Requirements:
-   • College admission assessments (if required).
-   • Contextual data and information about your educational background.
-   • Disclosure of unspent criminal convictions (if applicable).
- Post-Application Requirements:
-   • Interview preparation and attendance (if invited).
-   • Providing additional information or documentation (if requested).
-   • Receiving and responding to application decisions.

#### Application Deadlines

**Ucas Deadline:** October 15, 2025 for Oxford, Cambridge, medicine, veterinary science, and dentistry. January 15, 2026 for most other courses.

**International Deadline:** Same as UCAS deadlines, though some universities have different deadlines for international students.
