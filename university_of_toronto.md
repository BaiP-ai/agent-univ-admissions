## Canada {#canada}

### University of Toronto {#university-of-toronto}

**Website:** [University of Toronto](https://www.future.utoronto.ca/apply)

#### Admissions Requirements

- Academic Requirements: Minimum overall average of 80% in Grade 12 U/M courses (or equivalent) for most programs
- Academic Requirements: Specific program prerequisites, such as math, science, or language courses, may be required for certain programs
- GPA Requirement: Typically, a minimum GPA of 3.0 (or equivalent) is required for most programs
- Standardized Test Scores: SAT or ACT scores may be required for some programs, with minimum scores varying by program
- English Language Requirements: Proof of English facility, such as IELTS (minimum 6.5), TOEFL (minimum 100), or CAEL (minimum 70)
- Application Materials: Official high school transcript or equivalent, showing all completed and in-progress courses
- Application Materials: Official test scores, such as SAT, ACT, or TOEFL, if required by the program
- Application Materials: Letters of recommendation, if required by the program
- Application Materials: Personal statement or essay, if required by the program
- Application Deadline: Check Important Application Dates for specific deadlines, which vary by program and semester
- Transfer Credit Information: Available on the Transfer Credit Information page
- International Student Requirements: Additional documentation, such as a study permit, may be required for international students
- Non-Degree Student Requirements: Additional documentation, such as proof of enrollment, may be required for non-degree students

#### Application Deadlines

**Application Deadline:** January 15, 2026. Notification by April.

**International Deadline:** December 1, 2025 for international students requiring visas.
