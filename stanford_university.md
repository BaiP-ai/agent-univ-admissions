## US {#us}

### Stanford University {#stanford-university}

**Website:** [Stanford University](https://admission.stanford.edu/apply)

#### Admissions Requirements

- Meet the minimum high school graduation requirement: complete a minimum of 12 academic units in English, mathematics, science, social science, and foreign language.
- Earn a minimum GPA of 3.5 or higher in challenging academic courses, such as Advanced Placement (AP) or International Baccalaureate (IB) classes.
- Take the SAT or ACT exam and submit official scores: Stanford accepts both tests and has no preference for one over the other.
- Take SAT Subject Tests (optional but recommended): Stanford recommends submitting scores from two SAT Subject Tests, one in literature and one in math or science.
- Complete and submit the Common App or Coalition App online application, including the Stanford Questions and a $90 non-refundable application fee.
- Submit the School Report, which includes a counselor letter of recommendation and an official transcript, through the Common App or Coalition App.
- Submit letters of recommendation from two academic teachers, preferably from the 11th or 12th grade, through the Common App or Coalition App.
- Write and submit a personal statement and short responses to the Stanford Questions, which are part of the online application.
- Submit optional arts or creative supplements, such as music, theater, dance, or visual arts, if applicable.
- Apply for financial aid, if necessary, by submitting the CSS Profile and FAFSA forms.
- International students must submit additional requirements, including TOEFL or IELTS scores, if applicable, and certified translations of academic documents.

#### Application Deadlines

**Early Action:** November 1, 2025. Notification by mid-December.

**Regular Decision:** January 1, 2026. Notification by late March.
