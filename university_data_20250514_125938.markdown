## Australia {#australia}

### Australian National University {#australian-national-university}

**Website:** [Australian National University](https://www.anu.edu.au/study/apply)

#### Admissions Requirements

- Completed Common Application or Coalition Application
- Official high school transcripts
- Letters of recommendation from teachers (typically 2-3)
- Personal statement or essay
- Application fee or fee waiver request
- Minimum GPA requirement (typically 3.0 or higher)
- Standardized test scores (SAT/ACT may be optional)
- International applicants: TOEFL/IELTS scores if English is not your first language

#### Application Deadlines

**Application Deadline:** January 15, 2026. Notification by April.

**International Deadline:** December 1, 2025 for international students requiring visas.

### University of Melbourne {#university-of-melbourne}

**Website:** [University of Melbourne](https://study.unimelb.edu.au/how-to-apply)

#### Admissions Requirements

- Meet the academic requirements: completion of an Australian Year 12 or equivalent international qualification
- Achieve a minimum Australian Tertiary Admissions Rank (ATAR) of 85.00 or equivalent
- Satisfy the prerequisite subjects for your chosen course, if applicable
- Meet the English language requirements: IELTS overall band of 6.5, with no band less than 6.0, or TOEFL iBT overall score of 105, with a written section score of at least 24 and no section score less than 21
- Provide certified copies of academic transcripts and certificates
- Submit a personal statement or motivation letter, outlining your academic achievements, career goals, and reasons for choosing the University of Melbourne
- Provide references or letters of recommendation from academic or professional referees
- Pay the application fee, if applicable

#### Application Deadlines

**Application Deadline:** January 15, 2026. Notification by April.

**International Deadline:** December 1, 2025 for international students requiring visas.

## Canada {#canada}

### University of British Columbia {#university-of-british-columbia}

**Website:** [University of British Columbia](https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc)

#### Admissions Requirements

- Meet UBC's general admission requirements
- Achieve a minimum overall academic average of 70% in high school courses (or equivalent)
- Meet the specific admission requirements for your chosen degree and campus
- Demonstrate a minimum level of English language proficiency (one of nine ways to meet this requirement)
- Complete four or more consecutive years of full-time education in English in Canada (one way to meet English language proficiency)
- Provide proof of English language proficiency test scores (e.g., IELTS, TOEFL, CAEL)
- Meet the English Language Admission Standard
- Submit high school transcripts and diplomas (for high school students)
- Submit post-secondary transcripts and diplomas (for transfer students)
- Submit International Baccalaureate Diploma, if applicable
- Submit Advanced Placement (AP) exam scores, if applicable
- Write and submit a Personal Profile essay
- Submit all required documents, including transcripts, test scores, and language proficiency certificates
- Apply for a study permit (if international student) as soon as you receive your letter of admission and Provincial Attestation Letter (if required)
- Review and meet the specific admission requirements for your chosen degree and campus
- Meet the deadlines for application submission and document submission

#### Application Deadlines

**Application Deadline:** January 15, 2026. Notification by April.

**International Deadline:** December 1, 2025 for international students requiring visas.

- International Student Requirements: Additional documentation, such as a study permit, may be required for international students

### Imperial College London {#imperial-college-london}

**Website:** [Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/study/ug/apply)

#### Admissions Requirements

- Academic requirements: A-levels, International Baccalaureate Diploma, or equivalent qualifications accepted (check specific course requirements)
- Typical GPA: 3.2 or higher (varies by course and department)
- English language requirements: IELTS 6.0, TOEFL 92, or Cambridge Advanced English (varies by course and department)

- Application fee: £50-100 (waivers available for low-income students)

- Selection process: academic performance, personal statement, and references evaluated; some courses require interviews or admissions tests
- Deadlines: vary by course and department (check specific course deadlines)
- Application materials: submit through UCAS or directly to Imperial College London (check specific course requirements)
- Postgraduate taught and doctoral programs: additional requirements, including research proposals, CVs, and language proficiency tests
- International students: additional requirements, including English language proficiency, visa applications, and financial guarantees
- Visiting students: additional requirements, including language proficiency, academic transcripts, and course approvals

The PhD Studentships program at LSE provides students with advanced research training and the opportunity to contribute to original research in their chosen field, preparing them for careers in academia, research, or government. ### University College London {#university-college-london}

**Website:** [University College London](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/prospective-students/undergraduate/how-apply)

**Average ACT:** 15-25

#### Admissions Requirements

- Meet the A Level or equivalent entry requirements for the chosen
- Specific grade and subject requirements are shown on each programme page of the Undergraduate Prospectus
- International students must meet the international qualification entry requirements, which can be found by selecting the country or country of origin in the Other Qualifications section
- Typically, a minimum GPA of 3.3 or higher is required, but this may be subject to change
- English language proficiency is required, with a minimum of IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL 80
- Some programmes may require additional tests, such as the UCL Undergraduate Pre-Sessional or the International Pre-Master's
- Meet the programme-specific requirements, such as the Engineering Foundation Year eligibility
- Some programmes, like the Faculty of Medical Sciences, may have additional requirements, such as a genuine compelling reason for transferring
- Second-year transfer applicants must have covered similar content to the first year at UCL, at a standard judged equivalent, at their current higher education institution
- Applicants must apply by the UCAS equal consideration deadline to guarantee consideration
- Submit a complete application, including all required documents and information, by the specified deadline
- Some applicants, such as those required to complete compulsory national military service, may be able to defer their entry to UCL by up to two years
- Applicants with disabilities must provide supporting documentation and may be eligible for additional support and accommodations

**Ucas Deadline:** October 15, 2025 for Oxford, Cambridge, medicine, veterinary science, and dentistry. January 15, 2026 for most other courses.

**Website:** [University of Cambridge](https://www.undergraduate.study.cam.ac.uk/applying)

#### Admissions Requirements

- Academic Requirements:
-   • A strong academic record, typically with A*AA or AAA at A-level, or equivalent international qualifications.
-   • Meeting the specific subject requirements for your chosen course.
-   • Typically, a GPA of 3.7 or higher (on a 4.0 scale) or equivalent.
-   • Meeting the English language proficiency, typically IELTS 7.5 or TOEFL 110.
- Standardized Tests:
-   • Sixth Term Exam Papers (STEP) for Mathematics and Computer Science courses.
-   • University Clinical Aptitude Test (UCAT) for Medicine and Veterinary Medicine courses.
-   • Law National Aptitude Test (LNAT) for Law courses.
-   • Engineering and Science Admission Test (ESAT) for Engineering and Natural Sciences courses.
-   • Mathematics Admission Test (TMUA) for Mathematics courses.
- Application Materials:
-   • UCAS application, including a personal statement and academic reference.
-   • My Cambridge Application, including a transcript and any required written work or portfolios.
-   • College admission assessments, if required.
- International Requirements:
-   • International financial support, including tuition fees and living costs.
-   • Visas and immigration requirements.
-   • Language proficiency tests, such as IELTS or TOEFL.
- Additional Requirements:
-   • Contextual data, including information about your school and socio-economic background.
-   • Disclosure of unspent criminal convictions.
-   • Providing a transcript and disruption to your studies, if applicable.
-   • Mature student applications, if applicable.
-   • Second undergraduate degree applications, if applicable.
- Post-Application Requirements:
-   • Written work and portfolios, if required.
-   • Cambridge interviews, if invited.
-   • College admission assessments, if required.
- After You Apply:
-   • Application decisions and outcomes.
-   • Terms of Admission, including any conditions or requirements.
-   • Reconsideration eligibility criteria, if applicable.

**Ucas Deadline:** October 15, 2025 for Oxford, Cambridge, medicine, veterinary science, and dentistry. January 15, 2026 for most other courses.

### Harvard University {#harvard-university}

**Website:** [Harvard University](https://college.harvard.edu/admissions)

#### Admissions Requirements

- Academic Requirement: No specific GPA requirement, but a strong academic record is expected.
- Standardized Test Scores: Harvard requires the SAT or ACT. In exceptional cases, AP exam results, IB Actual or Predicted Scores, GCSE/A-Level Actual or Predicted Results, or National Leaving Exams Results (externally assessed) or Predictions can be accepted.
- Standardized Test Scores: English language proficiency examinations such as TOEFL, IELTS, and Duolingo cannot be used to meet the standardized testing requirement.
- Self-Reporting: Students may self-report their standardized test scores.
- Teacher Recommendations: Two teacher recommendations are required.
- School Report: A school report is required.
- Application Essay: A personal essay is required as part of the application.
- Supplement: The supplement includes five required short-answer questions, each with a 150-word limit.
- Application Fee: A non-refundable application fee of $75, or a Harvard-specific fee waiver code JH3S5QX9 for eligible students.
- Transfer Application Fee Waiver: Transfer applicants can request a fee waiver by following the instructions on the Transfer Application Requirements page.
- English Language Proficiency: Students may submit English language proficiency examinations such as TOEFL, IELTS, and Duolingo for review as part of their application materials.

#### Application Deadlines

**Early Action:** November 1, 2025. Notification by mid-December.

**Regular Decision:** January 1, 2026. Notification by late March.
