## Malaysia {#malaysia}

### Universiti Malaya {#universiti-malaya}

**Website:** [Universiti Malaya](https://study.um.edu.my/how-to-apply-bachelor-rsquo-s-degree)

**Average GPA:** 3.00

#### Admissions Requirements

- Academic Requirements:
-   • Foundation Programmes: Minimum of 5 credits in SPM/O-Level, including Mathematics and Science.
-   • Diploma Programmes: Minimum of 3 credits in SPM/O-Level, including relevant subjects.
-   • Undergraduate Programmes: Minimum of 2.00 CGPA in STPM/A-Level, or equivalent.
-   • Postgraduate Programmes: Bachelor's degree with a minimum of 2.50 CGPA, or equivalent.
- English Language Proficiency:
-   • IELTS: Minimum band 5.5 for undergraduate programmes, and 6.0 for postgraduate programmes.
-   • TOEFL: Minimum score of 550 (paper-based) or 79 (internet-based) for undergraduate programmes, and 600 (paper-based) or 100 (internet-based) for postgraduate programmes.
- Application Materials:
-   • Completed online application form.
-   • Certified true copies of academic transcripts and certificates.
-   • Passport-sized photograph.
-   • Payment of application fee (RM 150 for Malaysian, RM 300 for international).
- Application Deadlines:
-   • Foundation Programmes (Malaysian): First phase - 10 Feb 2025 - 14 Mar 2025, Second phase - 14 Apr 2025 - 18 May 2025.
-   • Foundation Programmes (International): 01 Mar 2025 - 31 May 2025.
-   • Diploma Programmes (Malaysian): 10 Feb 2025 - 30 Aug 2025.
-   • Diploma Programmes (International): 10 Feb 2025 - 31 May 2025.
-   • Undergraduate Programmes (Malaysian): First phase - 10 Feb 2025 - 14 Mar 2025, Second phase - 14 Apr 2025 - 18 May 2025.
-   • Undergraduate Programmes (International): 10 Feb 2025 - 30 Aug 2025.
-   • Postgraduate Programmes (Malaysian): 10 Feb 2025 - 30 Aug 2025.
-   • Postgraduate Programmes (International): 10 Feb 2025 - 30 Aug 2025.
- Additional Requirements:
-   • SATU Foundation Pamphlet (for SATU channel applicants).
-   • Proof of financial support (for international students).
-   • Health insurance (for international students).

#### Application Deadlines

**Application Deadline:** January 15, 2026. Notification by April.

**International Deadline:** December 1, 2025 for international students requiring visas.
