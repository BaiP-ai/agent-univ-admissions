## Malaysia {#malaysia}

### Universiti Malaya {#universiti-malaya}

**Website:** [Universiti Malaya](https://study.um.edu.my/how-to-apply-bachelor-rsquo-s-degree)

**Average GPA:** 3.00

#### Admissions Requirements

- Academic Qualifications:
-   • SPM/O-Level/IGCSE: Provide grades for each subject
-   • STPM/STAM/Matriculation/Foundation/High School/Diploma/A-level/Others: Provide transcript and certificate
-   • Minimum GPA of 3.0 (or equivalent) for most programs
- English Language Proficiency:
-   • IELTS: Minimum score of 5.0
-   • TOEFL: Minimum score of 500 (paper-based) or 60 (internet-based)
-   • MUET: Minimum score of Band 3
-   • English Language Proficiency test scores must be valid for at least 2 years
- Application Materials:
-   • Personal Details: Provide personal particulars and click 'SAVE & CONTINUE'
-   • Next of Kin Details: Provide details of next of kin and click 'SAVE & CONTINUE'
-   • Academic Qualifications: Provide information on academic qualifications and upload supporting documents
-   • English Language Proficiency: Select English Language Proficiency test, provide score, and upload certificate
- Application Submission:
-   • Create an account via http://apply.um.edu.my and proceed to create a new application
-   • Select 'Citizen' as nationality, 'Universiti Malaya's Open Channel (SATU)' as mode of implementation, and 'Undergraduate: Universiti Malaya's Open Channel (SATU)' as level of study
-   • Click 'APPLY' to confirm program selection
-   • Pay a processing fee of RM300 and save for future reference

#### Application Deadlines

**Application Deadline:** January 15, 2026. Notification by April.

**International Deadline:** December 1, 2025 for international students requiring visas.
