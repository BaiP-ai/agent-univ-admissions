## UK {#uk}

### University of Manchester {#university-of-manchester}

**Website:** [University of Manchester](https://www.manchester.ac.uk/study/undergraduate/applications)

#### Degree Programs

**MSc Data Science** (Master's, 1 year)

This program teaches students to extract insights from large datasets, develop predictive models, and create data visualizations to communicate findings. Students learn to work with various data tools and technologies, including Python, R, and SQL.

**BSc Computer Science** (Bachelor's, 3 years)

This program covers the fundamental principles of computer science, including programming, algorithms, computer systems, and software engineering. Students develop skills in designing, building, and testing software systems.

**PhD Physics** (PhD, 3-4 years)

This research-based program allows students to explore various areas of physics, including condensed matter physics, particle physics, and theoretical physics. Students work under the supervision of experienced researchers to produce original contributions to the field.

#### Admissions Requirements

- Academic requirements: A-levels or equivalent qualifications, with specific grades and subjects required for each course
- Typical A-level grades: AAA, AAB, or ABB, depending on the course
- International Baccalaureate (IB) Diploma: 36-40 points, with 6, 6, 6 at Higher Level
- GCSE requirements: English Language and Mathematics at grade C or above
- GPA: Not applicable, as the University of Manchester uses a UK-based grading system
- English language proficiency: IELTS 6.5 overall, with 6.0 in each component, or TOEFL iBT 90 overall
- Application form: Completed online application through UCAS (Universities and Colleges Admissions Service)
- Personal statement: A 4,000-character statement outlining academic achievements, interests, and career aspirations
- Academic reference: One reference from a teacher or academic advisor
- Predicted grades: Submit predicted grades from your school or college, if applicable
- Course-specific requirements: Check the course details for any additional requirements, such as portfolios, auditions, or admissions tests

#### Application Deadlines

**Ucas Deadline:** October 15, 2025 for Oxford, Cambridge, medicine, veterinary science, and dentistry. January 15, 2026 for most other courses.

**International Deadline:** Same as UCAS deadlines, though some universities have different deadlines for international students.
