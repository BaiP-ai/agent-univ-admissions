## UK {#uk}

### University of Manchester {#university-of-manchester}

**Website:** [University of Manchester](https://www.manchester.ac.uk/study/undergraduate/applications)

#### Degree Programs

**PhD in Physics** (PhD, 3-4 years)

The PhD in Physics program at the University of Manchester enables students to conduct original research in a wide range of topics, from condensed matter physics to particle physics and astronomy. Students develop advanced research skills, critical thinking, and problem-solving abilities.

**PhD in Computer Science** (PhD, 3-4 years)

The PhD in Computer Science program at the University of Manchester focuses on advancing the state-of-the-art in computer science, with research areas including artificial intelligence, data science, and human-computer interaction. Students develop expertise in designing, implementing, and evaluating complex computer systems.

**PhD in Biology** (PhD, 3-4 years)

The PhD in Biology program at the University of Manchester offers research opportunities in various areas of biological sciences, including ecology, evolution, and molecular biology. Students develop advanced research skills, critical thinking, and problem-solving abilities, and contribute to the advancement of biological knowledge.

#### Admissions Requirements

- Meet the academic entry requirements: A-levels, International Baccalaureate (IB), or other equivalent qualifications.
- Typically, A-level grades of AAA, AAB, or ABB are required, depending on the course.
- International students must meet the UK Visa and Immigration (UKVI) English language requirements, such as IELTS (6.5 overall, with 6.0 in each component) or TOEFL (90 overall, with 22 in each component).
- Some courses may require specific subjects or grades, such as Mathematics or Science for engineering courses.
- GCSE grades of 4 or above in English Language and Mathematics are typically required.
- Submit a completed UCAS (Universities and Colleges Admissions Service) application, including a personal statement and academic reference.
- Pay the UCAS application fee (currently £13 for a single choice, or £24 for multiple choices).
- Some courses may require additional admissions tests, such as the University Clinical Aptitude Test (UCAT) for medicine or the Law National Aptitude Test (LNAT) for law.
- International students may need to provide certified translations of their qualifications and transcripts, if not in English.
- Some courses may require a portfolio, audition, or interview as part of the application process.

#### Application Deadlines

**Ucas Deadline:** October 15, 2025 for Oxford, Cambridge, medicine, veterinary science, and dentistry. January 15, 2026 for most other courses.

**International Deadline:** Same as UCAS deadlines, though some universities have different deadlines for international students.
