## UK {#uk}

### Imperial College London {#imperial-college-london}

**Website:** [Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/study/ug/apply)

#### Admissions Requirements

- Academic Requirements:
-   • Undergraduate: A-levels, International Baccalaureate (IB), or equivalent qualifications
-   • Postgraduate Taught: Bachelor's degree with a minimum GPA of 3.0/4.0 or equivalent
-   • Postgraduate Doctoral: Master's degree with a minimum GPA of 3.5/4.0 or equivalent
- English Language Requirements:
-   • IELTS: minimum overall score of 6.5, with at least 6.0 in each component
-   • TOEFL: minimum overall score of 92, with at least 20 in each component
-   • Cambridge English: Advanced or Proficiency, with a minimum overall score of 191
- Application Materials:
-   • Personal Statement: a 1-2 page document outlining academic background, interests, and career goals
-   • Academic References: 1-2 references from academic professionals
-   • CV/Resume: for Postgraduate Doctoral applications
- Application Process:
-   • Online Application Form
-   • Application Fee: £50-£100 (waivers available for eligible students)
-   • Deadlines: vary by program, but typically fall in January, March, or April
- Admissions Tests:
-   • Undergraduate: may require admissions tests such as the Imperial College London Mathematics Admissions Test (MAT) or the Biomedical Admissions Test (BMAT)
-   • Postgraduate: may require tests such as the Graduate Record Examinations (GRE) or the Graduate Management Admission Test (GMAT)
- Interviews:
-   • May be required for certain programs, such as Medicine or Engineering
- Funding and Scholarships:
-   • Various scholarships and bursaries available for undergraduate and postgraduate students
-   • Students are encouraged to apply for external funding opportunities

#### Application Deadlines

**Ucas Deadline:** October 15, 2025 for Oxford, Cambridge, medicine, veterinary science, and dentistry. January 15, 2026 for most other courses.

**International Deadline:** Same as UCAS deadlines, though some universities have different deadlines for international students.
