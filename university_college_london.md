## UK {#uk}

### University College London {#university-college-london}

**Website:** [University College London](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/prospective-students/undergraduate/how-apply)

**Average ACT:** 15-25

#### Admissions Requirements

- Meet the A Level or equivalent entry requirements for the chosen
- Specific grade and subject requirements are shown on each programme page of the Undergraduate Prospectus
- International students must meet the international qualification entry requirements, which can be found by selecting the country or country of origin in the Other Qualifications section
- Typically, a minimum GPA of 3.3 or higher is required, but this may be subject to change
- English language proficiency is required, with a minimum of IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL 80
- Some programmes may require additional tests, such as the UCL Undergraduate Pre-Sessional or the International Pre-Master's
- Meet the programme-specific requirements, such as the Engineering Foundation Year eligibility
- Some programmes, like the Faculty of Medical Sciences, may have additional requirements, such as a genuine compelling reason for transferring
- Second-year transfer applicants must have covered similar content to the first year at UCL, at a standard judged equivalent, at their current higher education institution
- Applicants must apply by the UCAS equal consideration deadline to guarantee consideration
- Submit a complete application, including all required documents and information, by the specified deadline
- Some applicants, such as those required to complete compulsory national military service, may be able to defer their entry to UCL by up to two years
- Applicants with disabilities must provide supporting documentation and may be eligible for additional support and accommodations

#### Application Deadlines

**Ucas Deadline:** October 15, 2025 for Oxford, Cambridge, medicine, veterinary science, and dentistry. January 15, 2026 for most other courses.

**International Deadline:** Same as UCAS deadlines, though some universities have different deadlines for international students.
